Travel information

The questions travellers ask us most, answered in one place.

Murree and GaliyatAll year

Cool summers. Snow in Murree, Ayubia and Nathia Gali usually falls between December and February.

Hunza and SkarduApril to October

Blossom in April, green summers, golden autumn colour in late October.

Naran, Kaghan, DeosaiMid May to September

The road to Naran and upper Kaghan closes in winter. Kiwai and Shogran are good alternatives then.

Lahore and IslamabadOctober to March

Pleasant days. Summer afternoons can pass 40°C.

Visas

Most visitors apply online through the official Pakistan Online Visa System. Rules depend on your nationality, so check the portal before you book flights. We can provide a tour itinerary to support your application.

Permits for some areas

A few areas near borders, including parts of Neelum Valley, may need extra permission for foreign nationals. Tell us your nationality when you enquire and we will plan a route that works.

Money

Cash in Pakistani rupees is needed in the mountains. Cards work in cities and larger hotels. Carry enough cash before leaving Islamabad or Gilgit.

Staying connected

Local SIM cards need registration with your passport. Signal is limited in Deosai, Fairy Meadows and upper Neelum.

Mountain roads

Landslides and snow can close roads, especially in spring and during monsoon rain (July and August). We build a spare day into longer northern tours and check road conditions before each leg.

Flights north

Flights from Islamabad to Skardu and Gilgit save a long drive but depend on the weather. We keep a road plan ready if a flight is cancelled.

Altitude

Deosai and Khunjerab are above 4,000 metres. Climb gradually, drink water and tell us about any health conditions.

Respect local customs

Dress modestly, ask before photographing people, and remove shoes when entering mosques.

Clothing

  • Warm layers for the north, even in summer evenings
  • A light rain jacket
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • A scarf or shawl for mosque visits

Essentials

  • Passport or CNIC (needed at checkpoints)
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses for high altitude
  • A power bank
  • Basic medicines and motion-sickness tablets

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a tour?

Send us your dates, group size and the places you want to see, using any enquiry button or the trip builder. Our team discusses your request and prepares a complete package with transport, hotels and meals, then sends it to you with rates on WhatsApp or email. Once you are happy, we confirm the booking in writing.

Why are prices not shown on the website?

Every trip is different. Rates depend on your dates, group size, hotels, vehicles and meals, so our team prepares a price for your exact request and shares it with you directly.

Do you arrange school, college and university tours?

Yes. We plan study and leisure tours for students and staff, with suitable transport, group rooms and group meals. Ask about our special discounts for educational groups.

Can you arrange group meals and buffets?

Yes. We arrange breakfast, hi-tea, lunch and dinner for groups, including buffets at Ramada by Wyndham in Islamabad, Usmania Restaurant in Murree and our own Dervash Restaurant.

Do I pay on this website?

No. Nothing is charged online. Payment terms, including any advance, are shared with your written quote before you commit.

Can I change or cancel my trip?

Yes. Cancellation terms depend on the hotels and transport booked for your dates and are written on your quote. If roads or flights close because of weather, we work with you to reroute or reschedule.

Which hotels can you book?

Our own Al-Maaz Hotel on The Mall, Murree, our partner Hotel Felton on Bank Road, Ramada by Wyndham Murree, Mövenpick Centaurus Islamabad and partner hotels in Ayubia, Nathia Gali, Shogran and Naran.

Can I book transport only?

Yes. Share your pickup point, destination, date, number of passengers and luggage, and we will quote for a car, SUV or van with a driver.

Do you arrange trips for foreign visitors?

Yes. Tell us your nationality when you enquire so we can check whether any area on your route needs extra permission, and we can provide an itinerary for your visa application.

Tell us where you want to go.

Share your dates and who is travelling. Our team will come back to you with a plan.
