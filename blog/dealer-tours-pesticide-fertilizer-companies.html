Dealer and distributor tours for pesticide, seed and fertilizer companies

By the Discover Pakistan Tourism team · 9 November 2026 · 6 min read

For pesticide, seed and fertilizer companies, dealers and distributors are the business. A well-run reward tour builds loyalty that lasts longer than any discount. Because your guests are customers, the trip has to feel like hospitality, not a staff outing.

Time it around the season

Dealers are busiest during sowing and selling periods. Plan the tour for a window when they can leave their shops for a few days, and announce it early so they can arrange cover. Many companies link the tour to sales targets, so the dates are known at the start of the season.

Choose destinations by reward tier

  • Murree and the Galiyat: two or three days, suits large groups and first-time travellers to the hills.
  • Naran, Shogran and Kaghan (summer): four to five days with lakes, meadows and jeep trips.
  • Swat and Kalam: green valleys and rivers, good for families if spouses are invited.
  • Hunza or Skardu: a premium reward for top dealers, best with more days or flights.

Comfort comes first

  • Many guests travel from Multan, Bahawalpur, Rahim Yar Khan, Faisalabad, Sahiwal or Sukkur. Plan pick-ups by city and avoid very long single-day drives.
  • Choose comfortable vehicles with reclining seats and regular stops for meals and prayers.
  • Keep the pace relaxed, especially for older guests, and keep jeep trips optional.
  • Arrange familiar, generous Pakistani meals: a good breakfast, a proper lunch stop and a buffet dinner.

Make the awards evening count

A closing dinner with recognition for top dealers is often the moment people remember. Plan the stage, sound system, banners with your brand, photography and the order of awards in advance, and keep speeches short.

Keep everyone informed

  • Share a simple itinerary in Urdu and English on WhatsApp before departure.
  • Give each vehicle a group leader with a list of names and phone numbers.
  • Have one company representative and one tour coordinator reachable at all times.
