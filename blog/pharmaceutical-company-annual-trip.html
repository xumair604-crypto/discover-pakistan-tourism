Planning an annual trip or sales conference for a pharmaceutical company

By the Discover Pakistan Tourism team · 9 November 2026 · 6 min read

Pharmaceutical and medical companies often bring their field force together once or twice a year: to close a cycle, launch a product, train the team or reward top performers. Combining that meeting with a short trip out of the city keeps people engaged and gives the event a different energy.

Common trip formats

  • National or regional sales conference: one day of sessions, followed by a day of sightseeing.
  • Cycle meeting with a reward trip: a half-day meeting, then a relaxed two-night stay.
  • Top-performer incentive trip: a smaller group travelling further, for example Hunza or Skardu.
  • Product launch or training workshop: a focused programme where hall quality matters most.

Why Murree and the Galiyat work well

For teams based in Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Lahore, Murree and the Galiyat are close enough for a two or three day programme without losing a full day on the road. Hotels on and around The Mall can host meetings, the weather is cooler than the plains for much of the year, and there is plenty to do in free time: the Patriata chairlift, walks between Dunga Gali and Ayubia, and evenings on The Mall.

For a longer trip in summer, Naran and Shogran in the Kaghan Valley or Kalam in Swat offer a bigger change of scenery.

Planning the working day

  • Confirm the hall capacity for your seating style (theatre, classroom or round tables).
  • Ask for a projector, screen, sound system and a backup microphone.
  • Schedule tea breaks and lunch close to the hall to keep the day on time.
  • Plan sessions for the first full day, when everyone is fresh, and keep the last day free.

Keep it compliant

Most pharmaceutical companies follow an internal code of conduct, and Pakistan has ethical marketing rules for interactions with healthcare professionals. If your event includes doctors or other healthcare professionals, involve your compliance team early. We provide itemised plans and quotations so your team can review what is included.

Small details that make a difference

  • Arrange transport by region so each team travels together.
  • Prepare a name list for check-in and room keys before the group arrives.
  • Plan a closing dinner with awards: it is often the highlight of the trip.
  • Share a simple printed or WhatsApp itinerary with timings for every day.
