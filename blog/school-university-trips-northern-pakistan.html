Planning a safe school, college or university trip in Pakistan

By the Discover Pakistan Tourism team · 9 November 2026 · 6 min read

A study tour can be the most memorable part of a student's year. For teachers and organisers, it is also a big responsibility. These are the points we plan with every school, college and university group.

Good destinations for student groups

  • Islamabad: Faisal Mosque, the Pakistan Monument, Lok Virsa museum and Daman-e-Koh. Easy, safe and educational.
  • Taxila: the museum and ancient sites, often combined with Islamabad.
  • Lahore: Badshahi Mosque, Lahore Fort, Minar-e-Pakistan and the flag ceremony at Wagah.
  • Murree and the Galiyat: a first trip to the hills, with the Patriata chairlift and forest walks.
  • Naran, Swat or Hunza: for older students and university groups, with more days.

Transport

  • Use well-maintained coasters or buses with experienced drivers.
  • Do not travel on mountain roads at night.
  • Do a headcount at every stop, and keep the same seating for the whole trip.

Supervision and paperwork

  • Agree a teacher-to-student ratio with your institution, with female staff for female students.
  • Collect signed parental consent forms, emergency contacts and any medical conditions.
  • Carry copies of student ID documents and a list of names with parents' phone numbers.
  • Give every student the name and number of the teacher responsible for them.

Hotels and meals

  • Keep the whole group in one hotel, or nearby hotels, with teachers' rooms close to students' rooms.
  • Choose simple, fresh meals with plenty of water, and carry snacks for long drives.
  • Carry a basic first aid kit and know the nearest hospital on the route.
